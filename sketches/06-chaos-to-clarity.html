The route to clear

Three changes, not a giant transformation project.

Start with the information employees already need. Workhal gives it structure and a dependable publishing rhythm.

01 / Gather

Move the useful things in.

Collect guides, recurring answers, important dates and current notices from where they are scattered.

02 / Organize

Give each thing a purpose.

Separate timeless know-how from temporary updates and put each item in the right work area.

03 / Maintain

Keep one version current.

Managers draft, publish and retire information. Employees return to the same trusted place.

Questions before switching

No vague answers.

Do we need to move everything on day one?

No. Start with the guides and updates employees ask for most, then add structure at a pace the team can maintain.

Who can draft and publish workplace content?

Roles can separate content editing from publishing and broader workplace management.

Can temporary announcements expire automatically?

Announcements can have publishing and expiration dates so temporary information does not stay current forever.

Can employees use Workhal from a phone?

Yes. The employee experience is designed to stay focused and readable on a phone during the workday.
